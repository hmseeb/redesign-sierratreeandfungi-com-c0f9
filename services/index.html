Not sure which service you need?

Start from what you are seeing, and the entry point follows:

  • The tree looks sick, thinning, or off color: a tree health assessment
  • You are worried it will fail and hit something: a tree risk assessment
  • A county, city, or HOA asked for a report: arborist reports and permitting
  • You are buying a property with mature trees: the new homeowner assessment
  • Mushrooms or conks on the trunk or roots: fungal identification and pathology
  • Building or landscaping near protected oaks: development reports and the Oak Technical Report

The lines between these blur in practice, and that is fine: many visits combine a health and risk assessment in one, and most permit work begins with the same inspection. A site assessment with a written report starts at $500 whichever door you come in through, and if you are still unsure, call or text (530) 391-6100 and describe the tree; pointing you at the right service, including no service at all, costs nothing.
