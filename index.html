Sierra Tree and Fungi is the independent consulting practice of Christopher Hodge, an ISA Board Certified Master Arborist (WE-14385B), the highest credential the International Society of Arboriculture offers, and a TRAQ qualified tree risk assessor serving El Dorado, Sacramento, Placer, and Amador counties, California.

I have no removal crews and no financial incentive to recommend unnecessary work, just documented, conflict free advice from an ISA Board Certified Master Arborist with TRAQ qualification. I provide tree risk assessments, arborist reports, plant health care, and Oak Technical Reports, and I help property owners make informed decisions, preserve the trees they value, and steward their land.

The trees I work on are the ones that grow here: valley oak, blue oak, interior live oak, and California black oak through the foothills, and ponderosa pine, gray pine, sugar pine, incense cedar, and Douglas fir above them.

The underground network

Why “and Fungi”?

“Fungi are the drivers of so many positive and negative outcomes in tree health and viability.”

Why have fungi in my business name? Fungi are the drivers of so many positive and negative outcomes in tree health and viability. I have spent over fifteen years exploring forests studying fungal diversity and working as a consultant for an agricultural amendments company on mycorrhizal species.

Being a consultant arborist who has spent almost all my life in El Dorado County, California, I have been able to monitor individual trees for decades and watch how they respond to different wood decay fungi, weather patterns, shifts in land use, and fire. I have spent countless hours searching for spores with a microscope, reading research papers, and talking to a wide variety of experts who have spent their lives in the forest to grasp how we can keep our trees, forests, and ecosystems healthy.
